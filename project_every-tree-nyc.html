Every Tree NYC

[2026]

Every Tree NYC is an interactive software program that explores the over 1 million street and park trees across New York City's five boroughs. Users select one of the city's 250+ neighborhoods to watch a short animation reordering the trees by attributes such as genus, health, and diameter.

Constructed as part of Landscape Workshop, Data Through Design Exhibition 2026. On Display at BRIC House, Brooklyn, NY, March 21 - April 5, 2026.
